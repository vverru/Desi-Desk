FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these QR codes expire?
No! These are standard static QR codes. They never expire and encode the data directly without any third-party redirects or subscriptions.
How does the UPI QR code work?
It encodes the standard NPCI UPI specification (upi://pay?...). When scanned with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM, it automatically opens the payment screen with your UPI ID and payee name.
How does the WiFi QR code work?
Modern iOS and Android cameras read the WiFi credentials and show a "Join Network" prompt. Users can connect to your WiFi without typing complex passwords.
