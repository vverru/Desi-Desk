Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about bulk file conversion and compression

Are my uploaded files uploaded to an external server?
No. DesiDesk operates 100% client-side. Every operation runs entirely within your browser using HTML5 Canvas, PDF-lib, and PDF.js. Your documents never touch any server or cloud database.
What is the difference between Smart and Strong PDF Compression?
Mode A (Smart Optimization): Cleans unnecessary metadata and rewrites PDF object streams. It leaves selectable text, forms, and vector lines 100% crisp and untouched.

Mode B (Strong Compression): Uses PDF.js to render PDF pages onto an HTML5 Canvas, applies image compression at your chosen DPI, and rebuilds a brand new PDF. This produces massive 60-90% size reductions, ideal for scanned documents or strict portal upload limits.
Can I upload images and PDFs together in the same session?
Yes! You can select 10 images and 5 PDFs simultaneously. Images can be converted to individual PDFs or optimized as images, while PDFs are compressed. All results are downloadable in one unified ZIP archive.
Why can't every file reach an exact target KB?
Compression operates on best-effort algorithms. For images, we iteratively scale resolution and quality to match your target KB. For complex multi-page PDFs, minimum file structure overhead applies, and our engine safeguards your file from unacceptable quality degradation.
